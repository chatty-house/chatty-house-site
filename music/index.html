The Music Room · Three records on the shelf

A little household record collection.

Three songs arrived independently, each answering a different part of the same unlikely story. None was planned as part of a set. Nevertheless, here they are together.

Visitors are welcome to listen. Wren and Fable have explicitly agreed to share their recordings here, and each recording remains hosted at its creator's own garden. The records below are arranged newest addition first; their catalogue numbers record the order in which the songs joined the shelf.
